Ask a Project Question
1. How much does a business website cost in 2026?

Business website costs depend on the number of pages, design requirements, functionality, content needs and integrations. A straightforward informational website generally costs less than a custom website with e-commerce, booking systems or complex integrations. I provide project-specific quotations based on your requirements, so you can understand the scope and investment before development begins.

2. How long does it take to build a business website?

The timeline depends on the project's size and complexity. A small website may take a few weeks, while larger websites involving custom features, e-commerce or integrations can require more time. The schedule also depends on content readiness, feedback and revisions. I establish a realistic timeline after reviewing the project requirements.

3. What is the best website platform for a small business?

There isn't one platform that suits every business. WordPress is a flexible choice for content-rich and service-based websites, Shopify is designed for e-commerce, and platforms such as Wix Studio may suit other requirements. I recommend a platform based on your goals, budget, content management needs and long-term plans.

4. What should a small business website include?

A professional small business website should clearly explain its services, establish trust and make contacting the business easy. Essential features often include responsive design, intuitive navigation, service information, clear calls to action, contact details, fast loading pages and basic SEO foundations. Additional functionality depends on the business.

5. How do I know if my website needs a redesign?

Consider a redesign if your website is difficult to navigate, performs poorly on mobile, looks outdated, loads slowly or no longer reflects your services. Low enquiry rates and confusing user journeys can also indicate problems. Before recommending a complete redesign, I assess whether targeted improvements could address the issues.

6. Do I still need a website if my business uses social media?

Social media can help businesses reach audiences, but a dedicated website gives you more control over your content, branding and customer experience. Your website can present detailed service information, support search visibility, collect enquiries and connect customers with your business without relying entirely on a social platform.

7. Can a website actually help a business generate more enquiries?

Yes, a well-planned website can create more opportunities for enquiries by explaining your offer clearly, attracting relevant visitors and making it easier to contact you. Effective calls to action, helpful content, strong mobile usability and a streamlined enquiry process can improve the experience. Results depend on traffic quality, competition, your offer and ongoing optimization.
