Ask a Project Question
1. Do I need a new website or a redesign?

A redesign is usually appropriate when the existing website foundation can still support the business but the message, structure, visual experience or customer journey needs improvement. A new build makes more sense when the current system cannot support the required functionality or long-term direction.

2. Should I choose WordPress or Shopify?

WordPress is often a strong fit for flexible, content-led business websites and WooCommerce stores, while Shopify is designed specifically around managed e-commerce. The right choice depends on your business model, catalogue, integrations, team and long-term requirements.

3. Can you improve my existing website instead of rebuilding it?

Yes. If the existing platform and technical foundation are still suitable, the work can focus on the areas that are holding the website back rather than rebuilding everything unnecessarily.

4. What if I am not sure what platform I need?

You do not need to choose the platform before the project is understood. The business goal, required functionality, maintenance expectations and growth plans should determine the technology - not the other way around.

5. Can one project include a website, e-commerce and business integrations?

Yes. A project can combine public website pages, e-commerce, booking, CRM, email, analytics, client areas or other integrations when they genuinely support the same business objective.
