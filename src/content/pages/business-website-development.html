Ask About Your Website Project
1. How much does a professional business website cost?

The cost of a professional business website depends on the number of pages, design requirements, content, integrations and functionality involved. A straightforward service website generally requires less development work than a more customized website with advanced features. I provide a tailored quotation after reviewing your business goals and project requirements.

2. How long does it take to develop a business website?

The timeline depends on the project's size, content readiness, design complexity and required functionality. A smaller business website may take a few weeks, while larger or more customized projects can take longer. I outline the expected milestones and delivery schedule before development begins.

3. Which platform is best for my business website?

WordPress is often a strong option for businesses that need flexible content management and room to expand. Wix Studio, Squarespace or Framer may be suitable for other requirements. I recommend a platform based on your business goals, editing preferences, budget and long-term needs rather than choosing one system for every project.

4. Will my business website be mobile-friendly and SEO-ready?

Yes, responsive layouts and foundational on-page SEO considerations are part of the website planning and development approach. These can include clear page structure, descriptive headings, internal links and mobile usability. Specific SEO deliverables are agreed during project scoping, and no website can guarantee particular search rankings.

5. Can you redesign my existing website without rebuilding everything?

Yes. Depending on the condition of your current website, I can improve its layout, navigation, messaging, responsiveness or selected pages without necessarily replacing the entire system. If the existing setup has major technical limitations, I may recommend a more comprehensive rebuild.

6. Will I be able to update my website after launch?

Yes, when your project includes an editable CMS, you can manage content such as text, images and pages according to the platform's capabilities and your assigned permissions. I can also provide handover guidance, while ongoing maintenance or additional support can be discussed separately.
