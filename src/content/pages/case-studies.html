Ask a Project Question
What is a website development case study?

A website development case study explains a completed project's context, the client's challenge, the solution developed, the technologies used and the documented outcomes. It helps you understand the decisions behind the finished website, rather than only seeing screenshots.

2. What is the difference between Case Studies and Portfolio?

The Case Studies page provides detailed project explanations, including requirements, development approaches and results that can be verified. The Portfolio page offers a quicker visual overview of completed websites, with links to available live projects.

3. What types of projects are covered?

The featured work includes business website development, WordPress websites, website redesigns, product websites and other digital solutions. Individual case studies explain the specific services and functionality involved in each project.

4. Can you build a website similar to one shown here?

Yes, we can discuss a project with similar features or business objectives. Rather than copying another website exactly, I would recommend a solution tailored to your brand, target audience, content and required functionality.

5. Do all projects include measurable results?

Not always. Some projects have verifiable performance measurements, while others document completed deliverables, implemented features and improvements to the website structure or experience. I distinguish confirmed outcomes from objectives and avoid presenting estimates as proven results.

6. How do I discuss my own website project?

The technologies vary by project and may include WordPress, Divi, Elementor, WooCommerce, Shopify or other appropriate tools. Each detailed case study should identify the platforms and functionality actually used in that project.

7. How can I discuss a website project similar to these examples?

You can use the Contact page to share your business goals, existing website, required features and preferred timeline, or book a strategy call. I'll review the information and discuss possible next steps.
