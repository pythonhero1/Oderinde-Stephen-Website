FROM THE PROJECT BRIEF

An independent product website that complements Amazon — instead of depending on it.

Pipe Patch Pro needed its own web presence for product education, support and multiple purchase pathways.

BUSINESS GOAL

Build a standalone sales and support hub around three Pipe Patch Pro repair kits.

The client wanted Pipe Patch Pro separated from Amazon while still giving shoppers a direct route to the FMP 888 storefront and a WooCommerce option for direct orders.

SYSTEM DELIVERED

A WordPress Product Website with a focused landing page, product presentation sections and clear calls to action.

The build included Hostinger and WordPress setup, Homeowners, Contractors and Facilities Maintenance kit presentation, How It Works and Build Standards content, FAQ, Amazon review content, business email and WooCommerce onboarding support.

DELIVERED OUTCOME

Standalone product platform launched

pipepatchpro.net went live with dedicated product content, Amazon purchase links and a WooCommerce foundation. Direct WooCommerce payment activation remained dependent on the client completing banking and WooPayments onboarding.

DOMAIN MIGRATION & MIGRATE + BUILD

The domain move from Wix delayed the build — but not the product plan.

pipepatchpro.net was held at Wix until transfer eligibility, then moved to Hostinger. Development also required access troubleshooting and two-factor authentication coordination before WordPress installation could move forward.
