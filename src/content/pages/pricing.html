PRICING FACTORS

What Affects Your Project Price?

Website development, e-commerce and custom digital projects vary in complexity. Your final quotation reflects the agreed scope, platform, functionality, content requirements and delivery arrangements rather than a fixed package price.

Scale and complexity

The number of pages, product categories, content structures and custom requirements involved in your project.

Platform and integrations

Your CMS or e-commerce platform, payment systems, third-party services and required integrations.

Design and content

Custom design requirements, branding assets, copywriting, imagery and the availability of existing content.

Existing website condition

The current website's structure, technical issues, legacy plugins, migration requirements and data cleanup.

Functional requirements

Booking systems, user accounts, checkout processes, automation, permissions and tailored workflows.

Delivery requirements

Project dependencies, testing needs, review stages, approvals and agreed handover arrangements.

Ask a Project Question
1. Are the displayed prices fixed?

No. The displayed prices are starting estimates rather than fixed project quotations. Business Website Development starts from £450, E-commerce Development from £250, Website Redesign from £400, and Custom Digital Solutions & Automation from £700. Final pricing depends on the agreed project scope, functionality and requirements.

2. What is included in the starting price?

The starting price indicates the minimum entry point for a service, not a guaranteed list of features. Your quotation will explain the specific pages, design work, functionality, integrations and deliverables included, based on your project requirements.

3. Can I combine services?

Yes. A project may involve multiple services, such as website development, e-commerce functionality, redesign or automation. The requirements can be reviewed together to create a coordinated project scope and quotation.

4. Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. Existing websites can be reviewed for redesign, technical improvements, new functionality or integrations. The recommended approach depends on your current platform, website condition and project goals.

5. Are hosting, domains and third-party fees included?

Hosting, domain registration, premium plugins, paid integrations and other third-party subscriptions are considered separately unless your written quotation explicitly includes them. Any applicable costs should be identified before work begins.

6. How do I get an accurate quote?

Visit the Contact page and share your project goals, preferred platform, required features and any existing website or reference links. Your requirements can then be reviewed to prepare a scope-based quotation outlining the proposed work and next steps.
