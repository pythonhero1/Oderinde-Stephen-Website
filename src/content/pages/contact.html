Ask a Project Question
1. Can I contact you before I have a full project brief?

Yes. A summary of your business, goals and current challenges is enough to begin a conversation.

2. Do you work with businesses outside your country?

Yes. I work with remote clients and can discuss requirements online across different markets.

3. Can you improve an existing website instead of building a new one?

Depending on the existing website and your goals, a redesign or targeted improvements may be more suitable than a full rebuild.

4. What should I include in my initial enquiry?

Tell me the type of website or solution you need, your key goals, useful reference links, and any timeline or budget considerations.

5. How much does a professional website or digital project cost?

Project costs depend on the number of pages, design requirements, features, integrations and technical complexity. After reviewing your project details, I can provide a quotation based on the agreed scope and deliverables.

6. How long does it take to develop a website?

Development timelines vary depending on the project size, functionality, content readiness and revision requirements. A straightforward business website generally takes less time than a complex e-commerce store or custom digital system. I'll discuss a realistic timeline after reviewing your requirements.

7. Which website platforms and technologies do you work with?

I work with WordPress, Elementor, Divi, WooCommerce, Shopify, Wix Studio and other suitable website platforms and tools. The recommended technology depends on your business goals, content management needs, required features and long-term plans.
